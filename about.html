Our Story & Heritage

High-End Hair Design at Neighborhood Prices

Founded with a commitment to high-end hair design at neighborhood prices, Tony Hair Salon has become a trusted fixture in the Cary and greater Triangle community. Our philosophy is simple: hair care shouldn't be stressful or overly expensive. It should be a precise craft that makes you feel confident the moment you step out of our doors.

Our team stays continuously updated on the latest international hair trends, from modern cutting-edge texturizing methods to traditional premium chemical services. Whether you need a simple cleanup, a bold color transformation, or our specialized Magic Straightening, we take the time to consult with you one-on-one. Stop by today and discover the difference a precision cut makes!
