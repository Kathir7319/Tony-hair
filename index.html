Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about appointments, services, and salon policies.

We gladly welcome walk-ins based on our stylist availability! However, to minimize your wait time and guarantee your slot with master stylists Tony or Tom, we highly recommend scheduling your appointment in advance by calling (919) 650-2174.

Simply present your valid military ID, veteran ID, or dependent ID card to our team at checkout, and we will happily apply your exclusive community discount.

Our stylists have years of international experience handling a vast array of hair textures. We specialize heavily in difficult-to-manage thick hair, precision layering, texturizing cuts, Asian hair styling (like two-blocks and magic straightening), as well as classic American cuts.

We completely understand that life happens! We kindly ask that you give us at least a 24-hour notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment so we can offer that time slot to another client.
